About RADA AI

A transformation partner, not a technology vendor

RADA AI helps organizations transform how they operate and make decisions by combining AI strategy, workforce training, intelligent automation and executive management systems.

Nairobi HQ Private Limited Company Est. 2026

Our mission

To give business leaders Visibility, Control and Intelligence by transforming their operations with practical, enterprise-grade artificial intelligence — so technology serves the business, not the other way around.

Our approach

We enter an organization, understand how it actually operates, identify where AI creates value, train the people who will use it, implement the systems, and give management better intelligence — in that order.
