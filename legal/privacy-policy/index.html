Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

RADA AI Limited ("RADA AI", "we", "us"), a private limited company registered in Kenya (Company No. PVT-EY13DY87), with its registered office at Crescent Business Centre, Parklands Road, Parklands, Nairobi, is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to this website and of the organizations and individuals we work with. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the rights available to you, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable international data protection standards where relevant.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you submit through our forms — for example, name, role, company, work email, phone number, industry, organization size and the details of your enquiry.
  • Information collected automatically when you visit the site — such as pages viewed, referring pages, device and browser type, and approximate location, typically through analytics and cookies.
  • Information you provide when corresponding with us by email, phone or in person during an engagement.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries, diagnostic requests and training proposal requests.
  • To provide, operate and improve our services and this website.
  • To send confirmation emails and, where you have agreed, occasional updates relevant to services you have enquired about.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the basis of your consent (for example, when you submit a form), the necessity of processing to take steps toward a contract at your request, and our legitimate interest in operating and improving our services, balanced against your rights.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with service providers who support our operations (for example, hosting, email delivery, analytics and CRM providers) under appropriate confidentiality and data-processing terms, or where required by law.

5. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, consistent with the sensitivity of the information involved.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law or professional obligations.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing or withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled can be sent to contact@radaai.ai.
