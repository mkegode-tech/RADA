Clyde Mutsotso

Founder & Managing Director, CPA-K

A multidisciplinary business leader with a background spanning audit, tax, finance, law and business strategy. He began his career at the Kenya Revenue Authority before moving into entrepreneurship, building and scaling businesses across professional services, financial services and fintech — including as Managing Partner of Clyde & Associates, an audit and tax advisory firm. He founded RADA AI to bring the same practical, governance-first discipline to how organizations adopt artificial intelligence. He holds a CPA qualification, a Bachelor's degree in Economics and an MBA in Finance from the University of Nairobi, and is currently completing a Bachelor of Laws.
