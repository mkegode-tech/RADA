How a RADA engagement plays out

Illustrative scenario — mid-sized distribution business. Management could not see cash position or exception activity across branches without waiting on manual month-end reports. A RADA diagnostic prioritized a finance and operations use case, introduced a consolidated executive dashboard with automated exception alerts, and trained the finance team to maintain it.
