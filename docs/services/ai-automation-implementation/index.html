Workflow automation & process orchestration

Multi-step processes redesigned end-to-end, not automated one task at a time.

AI agents for defined business tasks

Agents scoped to specific, well-understood tasks rather than open-ended automation.

Integration with existing business systems

Working with the systems you already run, rather than requiring a rip-and-replace.

Document, email & data-processing automation
